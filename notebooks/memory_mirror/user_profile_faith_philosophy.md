# Peter — Faith, Theology, Philosophy

Companion to [user_profile.md](user_profile.md) (which is technical/Python-focused). This file covers the faith and philosophy dimensions Peter wants foregrounded.

## Faith identity

- **Catholic.** Intellectually engaged with the tradition as a live, rigorous body of thought — not as a private commitment to be apologized for in front of a scientific audience.
- **Reads primary sources directly.** Recurring touchstones:
  - **Augustine** — especially *Contra Academicos* (certainty / epistemology) and his theory of signs (*De Doctrina Christiana*)
  - **Aquinas** — per se vs. per accidens causal series, secondary causes, providence
  - **Luigi Giussani** — the *religious sense* as constitutive of reason itself; his notion of "reduction" of fundamental questions
  - **N.T. Wright on Paul** — recently came to find Paul "crazy smart"; struck by the outsized impact of occasional letters on the entire Western self

## Theological stance

- **Anti-fatalist.** Believes in providence AND prudence (Aquinas on secondary causes). "Trust in God" and "think carefully about this" are never in tension for Peter. When he says he'd trust God if we fell to our own folly, he means God ensures we reap what we sow — not that he should stop using his reason.
- Locates the roots of modern dangers (including AI-driven ones) in **classical disordered passions** — greed, anger, lust, pride — rather than in technology as such.

## Original philosophical project — symbol grounding → God / soul

**The argument:**
- Meaning requires a ground.
- Signs only ever refer to other signs ("no signs all the way down") — the regress can't ground meaning internally.
- What grounds meaning is **the mind's direct, present contact with intelligible reality.**
- The **soul** is that which has its symbol set grounded by the **Ultimate Ground (God).**

**Key refinement (Aquinas-flavored):** the grounding is a **per se causal series**, not merely per accidens. Meaning is sustained moment to moment by the mind's ongoing contact with reality, not just initiated by a past learning event. Remove the ground and meaning doesn't *lose its origin*; it *ceases*.

**Clean criterion for AI:** an embodied LLM with sensors still wouldn't have a soul, because sensory data is just more signs — another layer on the chain, not an end to the regress. No amount of sign-processing becomes the relation to the ground.

## Philosophical lineages he works in

Get these right — Peter will notice if they're misclassified:

- **Augustine** — predates the analytic/continental distinction; don't lump into "Continental"
- **Wittgenstein, Kripke** — analytic. Kripke's rigid designators, necessary a posteriori truths, reference as a real metaphysical relation (NOT a descriptivist cluster) are load-bearing for Peter
- **Saussure, Peirce** — semiotic tradition. Peter thinks Augustine asked the harder question (what makes the whole sign system possible at all?) 15 centuries earlier

## Where he stands re: the rationalist community (Yudkowsky et al.)

**Skeptical.** His specific objection:
- "Dissolving the question" is a descriptivist move that Kripke undermines at the foundation
- The rationalist habit of explaining questions away via "cognitive algorithms" / "evolutionary heuristics" is exactly Giussani's *reduction* — taking a question seriously enough to acknowledge it, then explaining it in terms beneath what it demands
- Finds **the hard problem of consciousness** and **the question of God** to be substantive, not confusions to be cleared up
