Peter has used em-dashes since high school as a deliberate stylistic choice and will not give them up. When editing his prose (applications, essays, blog posts), do NOT suggest removing em-dashes or flag them as an "AI tell."

**Why:** They're authentic to his voice, not an artifact. Flagging them is noise.
**How to apply:** When checking writing for AI-sounding patterns, judge by substance (specificity, hedged in-progress ideas, personal narrative) not punctuation. If em-dash frequency is genuinely worth a mention, note it as a reader-perception risk only, never as something to cut. See [[feedback-communication]].
