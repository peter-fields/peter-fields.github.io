# Substack — "A Physicist's Introduction to Catholicism"

Tentative title. Peter's public-facing writing project at the intersection of his Catholic faith and his physics/AI background.

## Planned arc

1. **Certainty and knowledge** — starts from Augustine's *Contra Academicos*. The nature of certainty and whether it's attainable. Targets the scientist's tendency to treat the numinous not as false but as *implausible and unworthy of consideration* — addresses that a priori dismissal head-on.
2. **The historical nature of Christianity** — death and resurrection of Jesus
3. **Salvation** — addressing evil, suffering, and death
4. **Morality**
5. **Sacraments**
6. **Devotion to Mary**
7. **Eucharist** (culmination)

## Signature line

> "Experiments never performed yield no results."

Pattern Peter likes: close each piece by giving the reader an *actual exercise*. Example exercise from the resurrection piece: contemplate an all-powerful being who enters creation and lets himself be wrongfully accused and killed — what does that reveal about the creator, and about wrongful accusation?
